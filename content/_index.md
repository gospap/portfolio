## Three disciplines, one engineer.

## Four steps, and the third one is the shortest.

<ol class="steps">
<li><strong>Find the seam</strong><span>Before any code, the question is which layer the problem actually lives in. Most briefs describe a symptom in the interface that is caused somewhere else entirely.</span></li>
<li><strong>Prototype the risk</strong><span>Build the one part that might not work — the shader, the sync, the sensor — before building the ten parts that certainly will. If it fails it fails in week one.</span></li>
<li><strong>Build</strong><span>The shortest phase, because the two before it removed the surprises. This is where scope discipline matters more than speed.</span></li>
<li><strong>Ship, then hold it up</strong><span>Live is the start of the job, not the end. Real traffic finds things no plan does, and the work is judged on what it does after that.</span></li>
</ol>
